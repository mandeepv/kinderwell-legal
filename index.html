Quick Help

Q: How do I access the demo mode?

A: Tap the "Save your progress" title 7 times quickly on the sign-in screen.

Q: How do I reset my password?

A: We use Google Sign-In and Apple Sign-In - password resets are handled through those platforms.

Q: Can I access my account on multiple devices?

A: Yes! Sign in with the same account on any device to sync your progress.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?

A: Manage your subscription through your iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions.
